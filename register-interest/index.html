What happens next

  1. Register your interest. It takes about 5 minutes.
  2. Tell us about your organisation. Share where you deliver, your current Support Coordination caseload, your staff and specialisations, and your registration status. What you share stays confidential.
  3. Talk with our team. We’ll call to walk you through the reform, what’s still unknown, and how a partnership could work for you.
  4. Shape the bid together. As government releases more detail, we’ll work with interested providers on regions, service model and partnering terms.
