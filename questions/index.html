Questions you might have

Government is still settling the detail, so some answers will firm up over time.

Register your interest

Free, confidential and non-binding. About 5 minutes.

What happens to the participants I support now?

Continuity matters to participants, and to us. Government is still designing how participants will move into the new service. We’ll push for arrangements that let participants stay with coordinators who know them, wherever possible.

What happens to my staff?

Your coordinators are a big part of why we want to partner with you. As a partner, you’d continue to employ your own team. We’d support them with induction, training and practice support through CoAct Academy.

I also deliver other NDIS supports. Can I still take part?

Government proposes that SCC providers don’t routinely deliver other NDIS-funded supports. How this applies to related organisations isn’t settled yet. We’ll help you understand the rules as they’re confirmed, and what they mean for you.

What will it cost, and how will I be paid?

We can’t confirm costs until government sets the funding model. The model has to leave partners better off than going it alone, with shared services that lower your costs. Partners should be paid fairly and promptly for the work they deliver, and shouldn’t carry the full risk of a government contract. We’ll be upfront about costs before you commit.

What do I need to contribute to the bid?

We’d lead the bid and do the heavy lifting. We’ll ask for information about your services, regions and capability. We’ll discuss any other contributions openly before you commit.

What will you do with the information I share?

We’ll use it only to understand your organisation and plan a potential bid. Only the small CoAct team working on the partnership will see it. We won’t share it with other providers without your permission, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Does registering interest commit me to anything?

No. Registering is free, confidential and non-binding. We’ll agree partnering terms with each provider before the bid.

Interested in tendering for Support Coordination and Connection with CoAct?

Register your interest to start the conversation. It’s free, confidential and non-binding.
