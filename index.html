Why now

What government has proposed so far:

  • Commissioned by region. Support Coordination funding moves out of participant plans. The National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) contracts providers directly, region by region.
  • Lead organisation favoured, for now. The consultation paper says the NDIA’s early thinking favours one lead organisation per region, delivering directly, through other providers, or both. A multi-provider model is still under consideration.
  • Far fewer providers. The paper is explicit that some current providers will not continue after mid-2028.

Questions you might have

What happens to the participants I support now?

Continuity matters to participants, and to us. Government is still designing how participants will move into the new service. We’ll push for arrangements that let participants stay with coordinators who know them, wherever possible.

What will it cost, and how will I be paid?

We can’t confirm costs until government sets the funding model. The model has to leave partners better off than going it alone, with shared services that lower your costs. Partners should be paid fairly and promptly for the work they deliver, and shouldn’t carry the full risk of a government contract. We’ll be upfront about costs before you commit.

Does registering interest commit me to anything?

No. Registering is free, confidential and non-binding. We’ll agree partnering terms with each provider before the bid.
