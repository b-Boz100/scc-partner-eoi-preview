Why now

The September 2026 consultation paper sets out a very different market.

Register your interest

Free, confidential and non-binding. About 5 minutes.

What government has proposed so far:

  1. Commissioned by region. Support Coordination funding moves out of participant plans. The National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) contracts providers directly, region by region.
  2. Lead organisation favoured, for now. The consultation paper says the NDIA’s early thinking favours one lead organisation per region, delivering directly, through other providers, or both. A multi-provider model is still under consideration.
  3. Two service levels. Support Coordination and Connection (SCC), and SCC+ for participants with very complex circumstances.
  4. Clear separation. SCC providers and workers aren’t expected to routinely deliver other supports funded by the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS).
  5. Far fewer providers. The paper is explicit that some current providers will not continue after mid-2028.
  6. Specialist and hard-to-reach areas served through partnerships. This includes Aboriginal Community Controlled Organisations and other specialist organisations.

What it means for you

A large regional contract asks for regional coverage, contract and performance management, quality assurance, systems, reporting and the money to carry the risk. Most local providers have never had to show all of these at once.

Prices for Support Coordination haven’t risen since July 2019. We’re pushing for a fair price, but we’re not banking on it. With thin margins and rising compliance costs, sustainability will come from lower overheads and steady volume. That’s hard to achieve alone.

If you want to keep delivering Support Coordination after mid-2028, now’s the time to plan your pathway, before the procurement opens.

Based on the Support Coordination and Connection Reform consultation paper (September 2026). Government is yet to confirm final settings, including the commissioning model, regions, eligibility and funding.
